# Tutorial film: shot list for recording the real product (production, screen video only)

Rule: **real FynHelp in production, recorded as video. No mock-up images, no stills of screens.** Use a **test firm** (`is_test`) with the invented client **Sundara Textiles Pvt Ltd, September 2026**, so no real client data is ever on screen. Sample files: `video-assets/hdfc_statement_sep2026.csv` and `video-assets/tally_daybook_sep2026.xml`. Expected: 7 bank lines read, 5 matched, 2 exceptions, closing balance 4,86,484.30.

Recording spec: 1920x1080, 30 fps, browser zoom 100%, cursor visible with a click highlight, no personal bookmarks or tabs, cookie banner dismissed first. One clean take per shot; pause 1 second before and after each action so it can be cut and sped up.

| Shot | Chapter | Real screen and action | Seconds |
|---|---|---|---|
| S01 | 1 Hire four agents | Website home page, slow scroll to the four-agents section | 12 |
| S02 | 2 Start in two minutes | Sign-up form: type name, tick "I am a Chartered Accountant", ICAI number appears, mobile, WhatsApp box ticked, work email, password, Create account | 35 |
| S03 | 2 | Firm setup: firm name "Mehta & Co", city, continue | 15 |
| S04 | 3 Add a client | Add client "Sundara Textiles Pvt Ltd" | 15 |
| S05 | 4 Extract | Documents: drag in the HDFC statement; the Extract agent working, then "7 transactions structured" | 25 |
| S06 | 4 | Upload the Tally day book | 15 |
| S07 | 5 Recon | Recon starts by itself; result "5 of 7 matched" | 20 |
| S08 | 6 Your call | Open the Review queue, then Exceptions: open one, show the evidence beside it, "Match with difference" and a note; set the other aside with a reason | 45 |
| S09 | 7 Narrate | Generate the monthly report; click a figure to open its source lines | 35 |
| S10 | 8 Sign-off | Partner signs off; "Report signed off" | 15 |
| S11 | 9 Chaser | Chaser: create a follow-up for a missing document; show the item and its activity | 30 |
| S12 | 10 Control room | Today screen; press Command K; Settings, Team, invite a colleague and choose a role | 35 |
| S13 | 11 Start free | Pricing page, then the sign-up page | 12 |

Not recorded live (they need a real phone and a real client, so they are animated by us, clearly styled as an illustration): the WhatsApp message a client receives and the client's no-login upload page. If you would rather show them for real, record your own phone screen during a test chase and upload it.
