Status: **draft for approval. Nothing has been produced yet.** Tell me what to change; I build only after you confirm.

## 1. Who it is for, and what they should feel
- **Viewer:** anyone in a CA firm: the partner, the manager, the junior who does the entries, the article assistant. No software or accounting-tech words they would not use at work.
- **After watching they can:** sign up, add a client, upload papers, see what matched, decide the odd ones, get the monthly report, get it signed off, chase missing documents, and invite their team. In that order, with nothing missing.
- **Feeling:** "This is simple. This is my month-end, and it is easier."
- **Rules for the words:** say "bank lines" not "transactions" the first time; "matching" not "reconciliation" (then say once: that is what you call reconciliation); "monthly report" then "MIS"; "helper" for each of the four agents (Extract, Recon, Narrate, Chaser). One idea per sentence. No jargon, no hype.

## 2. The story (one person, one month-end)
**Meera Rao, partner at Rao & Co** (invented firm). It is the 28th. Fourteen clients. Bank statements arrive as WhatsApp photos, Tally exports sit in email, and a junior is matching lines in Excel past 9 pm. Meera is not slow; the work is simply too manual.

Arc, in plain terms:
1. **The night before** (pain, 25 s): the late night, the chasing, the fear of one wrong number.
2. **A team of four helpers** (the idea, 30 s): Extract reads, Recon matches, Narrate writes the report, Chaser follows up. You decide, they do the legwork.
3. **Meera's month-end, step by step** (the tutorial, about 5 minutes): the real product, one client, real screens.
4. **The morning after** (payoff, 20 s): the report is signed off by 11 am; Meera is at her kid's school event. "You review and sign off. That is all."

Why this works: the viewer sees themselves in Meera's night, then sees the same steps they already know, just done for them. The four helpers give the product a face that is easy to remember and talk about.

## 4. Script (voice-over; short on purpose)
Tone: warm, calm, like a senior colleague showing you. English in plain words.

**0. The 28th**
It is the twenty-eighth. Fourteen clients. Bank statements arrive as WhatsApp photos. Tally files sit in email. And by nine forty at night, someone is still matching lines in Excel. Month-end should not take your evenings.

**1. Meet your four helpers**
FynHelp gives your firm four helpers. Extract reads the papers. Recon matches the bank against the books. Narrate writes the monthly report. Chaser follows up with clients for missing documents. They do the legwork. You make the decisions.

**2. Start in two minutes**
Sign up with your work email. Tick that you are a Chartered Accountant, and enter your ICAI membership number. Add your mobile number, and keep the WhatsApp box ticked so we can tell you if anything needs your attention. Then name your firm. That is it. Your fourteen-day trial begins when you upload your first document.

**3. Add a client**
Add your first client. A name is enough to start. You can add the details later.

**4. Give it the papers**
Upload a bank statement. A PDF, an Excel file, or even a photo works. Add the Tally day book too. Or let your client send it on WhatsApp or email, and it lands here by itself. Extract reads it in seconds and tells you what it found: seven bank lines, structured and ready.

**5. It matches by itself**
Now the matching. Each bank line looks for its partner in your books: same amount, same date, or a close match. Five of seven found their partner. That is reconciliation, done before you finished your tea.

**6. Only the odd ones need you**
Two lines did not match. That is where you come in. Open one: the bank entry is on one side, the nearest book entry is on the other, with the reason beside it. Match them, with a note on the difference, or set it aside with a reason. Next month, FynHelp remembers your choice.

**7. The monthly report in one click**
Press generate. Narrate builds a client-ready report: revenue, expenses and what is left. Click any number and you see the exact bank lines behind it. Nothing is a mystery, so nothing is a surprise when the client asks.

**8. Sign-off**
When you are happy, the partner signs off. One tap. The month is closed, and the report is ready to share.

**9. Chasing clients, without chasing**
Documents missing? Chaser sends a polite WhatsApp or email. Your client taps Upload, adds the file, no login, no app. Chaser stops the moment it arrives. If they need more time, they tap that, and you see the date they promised.

**10. Your team and your day**
Your Today screen shows what needs you. Press Command K to jump anywhere. Invite your team, and give each person the right role: partners sign off, juniors prepare. And if a document ever cannot be read, we tell you on WhatsApp, so you never have to go looking.

**11. Start free**
Try it with one client, free. After that, you pay only for the clients you are actively closing. Start at fynhelp.com.
