# FynHelp end-to-end tutorial: story, script, flow and showcase (v2, agentic)

Status: **v2 for final approval.** Decisions so far: English voice-over by ElevenLabs, female voice, firm "Mehta & Co", agentic positioning held strongly. Nothing has been produced yet.

## 1. The positioning the whole film holds
**"Four AI agents that close your month-end. You don't operate FynHelp. You manage it."**

Everything in the film proves this one idea, in the same four beats for every agent, so viewers learn the pattern without being told:
1. **A job.** Each agent has one clear job (a goal, not a feature).
2. **It works on its own.** It starts by itself and finishes by itself, with a visible "working" state.
3. **It shows its work.** Every result has evidence beside it (the bank line, the source lines behind a number).
4. **It calls you only when it needs a decision.** And it remembers what you decided.

The humans stay in charge: **agents prepare, the partner signs off.** The film says this plainly, because it is both true and what makes a partner comfortable.

Honest limits (we show only what is real today): Extract, Recon, Narrate and Chaser are the four agents. Recon starts by itself once the books are in. Chaser sends the reminders on a schedule and stops when the file arrives. Sign-off is a person's step, not an agent. FynHelp remembers a decision you made on an exception.

Words we use: "agent", "job", "hands off to you", "your call", "sign off". Plain words for accounting terms: "bank lines", "matching" (then once: "that is reconciliation"), "monthly report" (then once: "your MIS").

## 2. Who it is for
Everyone in a CA firm: partner, manager, junior, article assistant. After watching, they can sign up, add a client, hand over papers, see what the agents did, decide the odd items, get the monthly report, sign off, chase missing documents, and invite the team.

## 3. The story
**Meera Mehta, partner at Mehta & Co** (invented). It is the 28th. Fourteen clients. Bank statements arrive as WhatsApp photos, Tally files sit in email, a junior matches lines in Excel past 9 pm.

Arc:
1. **The 28th** (pain): the late night and the fear of one wrong number.
2. **Hire four agents** (the idea): each with a job.
3. **Month-end, run by the agents** (the tutorial): the real product, one client, each agent shown working, then handing off to Meera.
4. **The morning after** (payoff): the report is signed off by 11 am. Meera is at her child's school event.

The line that closes it: **"Four agents did the work. You made the calls."**

## 5. Script (voice-over)
Tone: calm, warm, confident. A senior colleague, not an announcer. Short sentences.

**0. The 28th**
It is the twenty-eighth. Fourteen clients. Bank statements arrive as WhatsApp photos. Tally files sit in email. And at nine forty at night, someone is still matching lines in Excel. What if month-end ran itself, and you only signed off?

**1. Hire four agents**
Meet your new team. Four AI agents, each with one job. Extract reads your papers. Recon matches the bank against the books. Narrate writes the monthly report. Chaser follows up with your clients. They start on their own. They show their work. And they call you only when a decision is yours. You don't operate FynHelp. You manage it.

**2. Start in two minutes**
Sign up with your work email. Tick that you are a Chartered Accountant, and add your ICAI membership number. Add your mobile, and keep the WhatsApp box ticked, so your agents can reach you when something needs you. Name your firm. Your fourteen-day trial starts when you upload your first document.

**3. Add a client**
Add your first client. A name is enough to start. Details can come later.

**4. Extract reads the papers**
Hand over the papers. A bank statement as a PDF, an Excel file, or even a photo. Add the Tally day book. Or let your client send it on WhatsApp or email, and it lands here by itself. Extract gets to work. A few seconds later, it reports back: seven bank lines, read and structured.

**5. Recon matches on its own**
You didn't press anything. The moment the books were in, Recon started. Each bank line looks for its partner in your books: same amount and date, or a close match. Five of seven found theirs. That is reconciliation, finished before your tea goes cold.

**6. Your call on the odd ones**
Two lines didn't match. Recon doesn't guess. It hands them to you, with the evidence beside each one: the bank entry on one side, the nearest book entry on the other, and the reason. Match them with a note on the difference, or set one aside with a reason. Your call. And next month, FynHelp remembers it.

**7. Narrate writes the report**
Now Narrate. One click, and your client-ready monthly report is written: revenue, expenses, what is left. Click any number, and you see the exact bank lines behind it. Nothing is a mystery. So nothing is a surprise when the client asks.

**8. Partner signs off**
The agents prepare. The partner decides. When you are happy, sign off, in one tap. The month is closed.

**9. Chaser follows up for you**
Documents missing? Chaser messages your client on WhatsApp or email. The client taps Upload, adds the file. No login, no app. The moment it arrives, Chaser stops. If your client needs more time, they tap that, and you see the date they promised. You never had to chase.

**10. Your team and your control room**
Your Today screen is your control room: what the agents finished, and what needs you. Press Command K to jump anywhere. Invite your team, and give each person the right role: partners sign off, juniors prepare. And if a document ever can't be read, your agents tell you on WhatsApp, so you never have to go looking.

**11. Start free**
Four agents did the work. You made the calls. Try it with one client, free. After that, you pay only for the clients you are actively closing. Start at fynhelp.com.
